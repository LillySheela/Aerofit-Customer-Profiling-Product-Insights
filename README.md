# Aerofit-Customer-Profiling-Product-Insights

This project analyzes customer demographics and treadmill purchase patterns for a global fitness equipment retailer. Using descriptive statistics, probability models, and visualizations, the analysis provides actionable insights into **customer segments, income groups, and product preferences**.

## 📊 Dataset

* \~180 customer records
* Attributes: Age, Gender, Marital Status, Income, Fitness Rating, and Treadmill Model Purchased
* Source: Provided business case dataset

## 🎯 Objectives

* Profile customers by demographics and income levels
* Apply descriptive statistics (mean, variance, skewness, kurtosis) to explore data distribution
* Use **probability & conditional probability** to analyze product preferences across segments
* Recommend strategies for targeted marketing and product positioning

## 🔍 Key Insights

* \~80% of customers are **young adults (18–35)** with **low-to-medium income**, preferring entry/mid-range treadmills
* Premium models are primarily purchased by **high-income male professionals** (82% male, 52% high-income)
* Identified distinct product-market fit across income and demographic groups
* Recommendations: Focus marketing of entry models on budget-conscious youth, while targeting premium campaigns at affluent professionals

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Python**: pandas, numpy, seaborn, matplotlib
* **Statistics & Probability**: descriptive statistics, marginal & conditional probability
* Colab Notebook for analysis and visualization
